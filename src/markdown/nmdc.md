## Overview

The NMDC, initiated in 2019 by the Department of Energy (DOE) Biological and Environmental Research (BER) program, is a large-scale effort to advance, standardize, and publicize microbiome research. The project converts fragmented multi-omics data into findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable scientific assets. Starting in 2025, I took over as the primary Software Engineer and UX Designer for the NMDC Data Portal. The Data Portal is the frontdoor for accessing and discovering high-quality microbiome data from samples collected around the world.
