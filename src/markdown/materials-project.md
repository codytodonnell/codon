## Overview

The Materials Project is a large-scale initiative to disseminate materials information to researchers and industry professionals across the materials science domain. The project is directed by Dr. Kristin Persson and is carried out by her group at the Lawrence Berkeley National Lab. From 2020 to 2022 I was the team's lead Web Developer where I helped modernize the front-end infrastructure and re-design the user experience of the Materials Project web apps.
